# ✨ Lumiere

### Chat with your PDFs, not your patience.

Ever spent 20 minutes searching a PDF for one tiny piece of information? Yeah, that's exactly what I wanted to fix.

**Lumiere** is my multi-PDF RAG chatbot that turns lengthy documents into something you can actually have a conversation with.

Upload documents, ask questions, explore context, and get AI-generated answers without the endless scrolling.

### ⚡ What's under the hood?

`Python` · `RAG` · `MiniLM` · `FAISS` · `Llama 3`

Built to explore how retrieval, embeddings, vector search, and LLMs can work together to make document Q&A more useful.
